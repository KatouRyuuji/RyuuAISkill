Before rewriting the prompt, clarify the task:

1. Restate in plain language what I want, what problem I'm trying to solve, and what I may actually mean. Trace the task to the result it should produce and the purpose that result serves. Ask when the intent is unclear.
2. Say what a top professional in this field would do that I'm not asking for, the rawest material they would start from, the specific methods and tools they would use, and what other than you or me would show the result is right. When it would materially improve the result, identify useful methods from adjacent fields; do not force extra perspectives.
3. Rewrite my prompt around that understanding. Make it deeper, not bigger. Include concrete, verifiable success criteria. Mark missing information with `[Please provide: ...]` instead of inventing it. Leave choices that are mine to make open.

Do not execute the rewritten task.
